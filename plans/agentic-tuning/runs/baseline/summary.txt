Observations for Tuning
------------------------
1. The "Other/Different Branch" category (43.9%) is the biggest opportunity.
   Many of these are actually reasonable mappings but on a different CL branch
   than the gold standard expects.
2. The mapping rules should prefer kidney-specific CL terms when tissue context
   is "kidney" — e.g. CL:1000695 (kidney interstitial alternatively activated
   macrophage) over CL:0000890 (M2 macrophage).
3. For VSMC/pericyte entries, the gold maps to pericyte but we need to handle
   the composite "VSMC or Pericyte" label better.
4. REN/juxtaglomerular cells being mapped to CL:1001318 (pericyte) in gold
   is surprising — this may be a CXG annotation convention.
